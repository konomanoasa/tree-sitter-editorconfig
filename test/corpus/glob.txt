==================
Nested alternatives and numeric ranges preserve ownership
==================
[{a,{1..3}}]

---

(document
  (section
    header: (section_header
      opening: (section_open)
      name: (pattern
        (alternation
          opening: (brace_open)
          (pattern (glob_literal))
          (alternative_separator)
          (pattern
            (numeric_range
              opening: (brace_open)
              lower: (integer)
              (range_separator)
              upper: (integer)
              closing: (brace_close)))
          closing: (brace_close)))
      closing: (section_close)
      (line_ending))))

==================
Set contents are literal
:cst
==================
[[!a-z*{1..2}\/]]
---

0:0  - 0:17   document
0:0  - 0:17     section
0:0  - 0:17       header: section_header
0:0  - 0:1          opening: section_open `[`
0:1  - 0:16         name: pattern
0:1  - 0:16           character_set
0:1  - 0:2              opening: set_open `[`
0:2  - 0:3              negation: set_negation `!`
0:3  - 0:15             set_text `a-z*{1..2}\\/`
0:15 - 0:16             closing: set_close `]`
0:16 - 0:17         closing: section_close `]`
